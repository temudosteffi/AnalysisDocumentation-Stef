WHAT THE COMPONENT SET SHOWS

Seven hardcoded QFrame steps

Stage 6 runs a fixed inner chain — cBuildCapEnrolls → cBuildCapAffils → cBuildCapAmount → cCapFundAllocation → cCapFundAdjustment → cStoreCapVoucher → cCapPayment — hardcoded in CapitationActivity, not configurable via the process-step table.

One PaymentID, shared by every voucher

cBuildCapEnrolls pre-allocates a PaymentID from QCSIDB IDSequence (idtype 4) before any voucher is written. All of a provider's vouchers share it (fan-in); reuse on restart is the primary-key collision surface at Stage 7.

The data tier spans five databases

Control & audit in PlanIntegration, sequence in QCSIDB, business reads/writes in Plandata_Parallel, rate staging in ETLStageDB, and crosswalks / supplemental income in HealthPAS_Common.
